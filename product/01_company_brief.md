# Stacc — Company Brief
**Version 3.0 | March 2026**

---

## The Problem

Every year, thousands of Africans complete data courses. They earn certificates, update LinkedIn, apply to jobs. Nothing happens.

The gap between "I learned data" and "I got hired in data" is not knowledge — it's proof. Employers don't trust certificates. They trust portfolios, shipped products, and verifiable work history. In 2026, with AI reshaping every data role, the proof requirement is higher and more specific than ever.

Most learners don't know what to study next, build tutorial projects no one uses, have no accountability structure, and cannot demonstrate what they actually know under live questioning.

**The result:** A generation of capable people permanently stuck in learning mode, in a market that has already moved on.

---

## The Solution

**Stacc is where data learners become Builders.**

We don't create content. We don't sell courses. We do three things:

| What | How |
|------|-----|
| Structure the path | A curated, AI-integrated skill roadmap of the best free resources, organized into a clear, visual progression |
| Force production | Real products, real teams, real users — not tutorial work |
| Build proof | A verified GitHub work history, deployed product, and interview Readiness Score that speak without explanation |

By the time you reach Prod, you don't hope for a job. You are undeniably ready for one — and you have the artifacts to prove it.

## Member Identity

Every person in the Stacc ecosystem has a clear identity tied to where they are in the journey. These are not titles — they are stages of proof.

| Stage | Member Name | What They Are |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| Dev | **Dev** | A learner building foundations and community credibility |
| Staging | **Stagee** | A contributor building real data and AI features on live Stacc products |
| Prod | **Builder** | A product maker who has shipped something real people actively use |
| Pro 1:1 | **Pro Member** | A mentorship client on an accelerated, personalized track |
| Post-Prod | **Builder Alumni** | A Builder who has graduated and remains active in the ecosystem |

> Builders and Prod members are the same thing. There is no separate "Builder" category above Prod. You become a Builder by completing Prod.

### Cohort Abbreviations

| Program | Format | Examples |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Staging cohorts | S + number | S1, S2, S3 |
| Prod cohorts | P + number | P1, P2, P3 |

Dev has no cohort numbering — it is always open and continuous.

**Usage in context:**
- *"She's a Stagee in S2"*
- *"He graduated as a Builder in P1"*
- *"We're opening applications for S3 next month"*

## Visual Identity

Stacc's aesthetic is **Modern Technical Brutalism** — dark, high-contrast, and data-dense. The feel is mission control: built for engineers, by engineers. Structure and function over decoration. Visual interest comes from functional elements — glowing status indicators, monospaced data readouts, structured bento grids — not illustrations or decorative flourishes.

Full specifications are in `04_design_system.md`.
